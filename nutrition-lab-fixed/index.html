🔬 About Nutrition Lab

Nutrition Lab was built for people who take their fitness seriously. We believe that great results come from understanding your body through data — not guesswork.

Our Mission

To make science-backed nutrition and fitness tracking accessible to everyone, from first-time gym-goers to seasoned athletes. Every feature is designed around one goal: helping you perform better.

What We Built

A full-stack platform that combines a precision macro tracker, AI-powered coaching, workout logging, hydration tracking, and progress analytics — all in one place, with no bloat.

The Stack

Built with Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks were harmed in the making of this app.

The Team

We're a small team of engineers and fitness enthusiasts who got tired of nutrition apps that felt like they were designed in 2012. So we built something better.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and fitness data such as weight, nutrition logs, and workout history. We also collect usage data to improve the app experience.

Your data is used exclusively to power your personal dashboard, generate AI coaching insights, and track your progress over time. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
